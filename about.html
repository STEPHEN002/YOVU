About Youth Voice Uganda

A community-based organisation born from Northern Uganda, fighting for youth, health, rights, and livelihoods since 2019.

Our Story

Who We Are

Youth Voice Uganda (VOYU) is a registered Community-Based Organisation (CBO) founded on 5th July 2019, based in Latwol Cell, Padibe Town Council, Lamwo District, Uganda. Registration Number: CBO L/CD/109972.

We were established in response to the urgent need for a locally driven voice for youth and vulnerable groups in Northern Uganda — a region still navigating the long-term impacts of conflict, poverty, and social inequality.

Our name says it all: we are the voice of young people who have long been overlooked. We advocate, train, sensitise, and mobilise communities to create lasting, sustainable change from within.

Led by Executive Director Abalo Doris and a passionate team of community champions, VOYU works every day to ensure that youth, women, and vulnerable populations can access health services, protect their rights, and build economic futures.

Our Purpose

Vision, Mission & Goal

🌍

Vision

A justifiable society with health and economic stability — where every youth and vulnerable person lives with dignity, rights, and opportunity.

📢

Mission

To advocate for youth and other vulnerable groups on health, human rights protection, and economic livelihood in Uganda.

🏆

Overall Goal

To improve the quality of life of youth and vulnerable groups through integrated advocacy, health, rights, and economic empowerment programs in Northern Uganda.

Objectives

What We Set Out To Do

Four clear objectives guide all our programs and community activities.

01

Human Rights Advocacy: Promote and protect the fundamental rights of youth, women, and other vulnerable groups through awareness campaigns, legal support, community mobilisation, and engagement with duty-bearers at local and national levels.

02

Socio-Economic Training: Empower youth and vulnerable groups with practical business skills, vocational training, entrepreneurship development, and financial literacy to build sustainable livelihoods and economic independence.

03

Health Sensitisation & Medical Support: Improve community health outcomes through health education programs, medical support, referral systems, and outreach activities targeting HIV/AIDS, maternal health, nutrition, and general wellbeing.

04

Adolescent Health: Provide comprehensive adolescent health programs covering sexual and reproductive health, mental wellbeing, life skills, and safe spaces where young people can access information and support without stigma.

Core Values

What Guides Us

These values shape every decision, every program, and every interaction we have with our communities.

📢 Youth Voice

We believe young people must be heard, included, and empowered to lead change in their own communities.

⚖️ Human Dignity

Every person has inherent rights and deserves to be treated with respect, equality, and fairness regardless of their background.

🤝 Community-Centred

We design and deliver programs with communities, not for them — local participation is the foundation of everything we do.

🔍 Accountability

We are transparent with our partners, donors, beneficiaries, and communities about how resources are used and what results are achieved.

❤️ Compassion

We serve with empathy, understanding the unique challenges faced by youth, women, and vulnerable groups in post-conflict Northern Uganda.

🌱 Sustainability

We invest in long-term capacity building so that communities can sustain positive change long after our programs end.
