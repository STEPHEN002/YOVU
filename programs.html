Our Programs

Four integrated program areas addressing the root challenges of youth and vulnerable communities in Northern Uganda.

Integrated Approach

Programs Built With Communities

Youth Voice Uganda designs and delivers programs that are rooted in community needs, informed by local realities, and driven by the voices of the young people and vulnerable groups we serve. Our four program areas are interconnected — rights enable economic participation, health enables learning, and livelihoods sustain communities.

⚖️
Program 01

Human Rights Protection & Advocacy

We believe every youth and vulnerable person in Uganda deserves to know, claim, and defend their rights. This program focuses on building legal awareness, community advocacy, and accountability systems that protect the most marginalised.

  • Community human rights awareness campaigns and workshops
  • Legal aid referrals and support for vulnerable individuals
  • Engagement with local government, police, and duty-bearers
  • Child rights protection and safeguarding initiatives
  • Gender-based violence (GBV) prevention and response
  • Training of community human rights champions and defenders
  • Advocacy for policy change at district and national level
💼
Program 02

Socio-Economic Training & Livelihoods

Economic empowerment is central to sustainable change. This program equips youth, women, and vulnerable groups with practical skills and knowledge to build dignified, self-sustaining livelihoods in Lamwo District and beyond.

  • Business skills training — planning, management, marketing
  • Vocational and technical skills training programs
  • Financial literacy, saving, and investment education
  • Village Savings and Loan Associations (VSLAs) support
  • Entrepreneurship mentorship and startup guidance
  • Agricultural skills and agri-business development
  • Linkage to markets, micro-finance, and business networks
🏥
Program 03

Health Sensitisation & Medical Support

Access to health information and services saves lives. VOYU runs community health programs that bring education, support, and referral services directly to communities who would otherwise go without.

  • HIV/AIDS awareness, testing promotion, and prevention
  • Maternal and child health education and support
  • Community health outreach and mobile health camps
  • Medical referral system to health facilities
  • Nutrition education and food security programs
  • Water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH) promotion
  • Mental health awareness and psychosocial support
  • Training of community health volunteers
🌱
Program 04

Adolescent Health & Youth Development

Young people need safe spaces, accurate information, and life skills to make healthy decisions. Our adolescent health program addresses the unique needs of youth aged 10–24, creating an environment of trust, learning, and empowerment.

  • Sexual and reproductive health (SRH) education
  • Teen pregnancy prevention and response programs
  • Life skills training — decision-making, communication, leadership
  • Safe spaces / youth clubs for open discussion
  • Peer education and youth-to-youth mentorship
  • School-based health and rights sensitisation
  • Substance abuse awareness and prevention
  • Psychosocial support for youth in distress
