Program Staff

Our Program Officers

Dedicated field officers who work directly with communities across Lamwo District every day.

⚖️

Human Rights Officer

Rights & Advocacy

Leads community rights awareness campaigns, supports GBV survivors, and engages with duty-bearers on behalf of vulnerable community members.

💼

Livelihoods Officer

Economic Empowerment

Designs and delivers business skills and vocational training programs, manages VSLA groups, and links youth to market and financial opportunities.

🏥

Health Officer

Health & Medical

Coordinates health sensitisation programs, mobile health camps, and referral systems, working closely with local health facilities and community health workers.

🌱

Adolescent Health Officer

Youth Development

Runs adolescent health programs, manages youth safe spaces, trains peer educators, and provides life skills training to young people across the district.

📊

M&E Officer

Monitoring & Evaluation

Tracks program data, prepares donor reports, and ensures evidence-based learning is integrated into all VOYU programs and decision-making.

📣

Communications Officer

Communications

Manages VOYU's communications, social media, documentation, and donor reporting — amplifying the voices and stories of the communities we serve.
