September 2026 Human Rights

VOYU Conducts Community Human Rights Awareness Workshop in Padibe

Youth Voice Uganda held a successful community human rights awareness workshop in Padibe Town Council, reaching over 80 community members with information on their legal rights, GBV response pathways, and how to access justice. Participants included youth, women, and community leaders.

August 2026 Economic

30 Youth Complete Business Skills and Entrepreneurship Training in Lamwo

Thirty young people — 18 women and 12 men — graduated from VOYU's 6-week business skills training program in Lamwo District. Participants gained practical skills in business planning, financial management, and marketing, and will be supported to access startup capital through village savings groups.

July 2026 Health

Free Health Camp Serves Over 150 Community Members in Remote Villages

In partnership with local health workers, Youth Voice Uganda organised a mobile health camp in three remote sub-counties of Lamwo District. Over 150 community members accessed free health consultations, HIV testing, family planning services, and malaria treatment. Thirteen individuals were referred for further care.

June 2026 Adolescent Health

New Youth Safe Space Opened in Padibe — A Hub for Adolescent Health

Youth Voice Uganda officially opened a new youth-friendly safe space in Padibe Town Council, providing adolescents aged 10–24 with a confidential environment to access sexual and reproductive health information, counselling, life skills training, and peer support every week.

VOYU Engages Lamwo District Local Government on Youth-Friendly Policies

Youth Voice Uganda met with Lamwo District Local Government officials to present community findings on barriers to youth employment, health access, and rights violations. The meeting resulted in commitments to include youth representatives in key district planning processes.
