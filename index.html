What We Do

Our Core Programs

Four integrated focus areas tackling the root challenges faced by youth and vulnerable communities in Northern Uganda.

⚖️

Human Rights Advocacy

Promoting and protecting the rights of youth, women, and other vulnerable groups through awareness, legal support, and community mobilisation.

Learn more →
💼

Socio-Economic Training

Building sustainable livelihoods through business skills, vocational training, entrepreneurship, and financial literacy programs for youth and women.

Learn more →
🏥

Health & Medical Support

Sensitisation on health programs, medical support, referral systems, and community health outreach targeting the most vulnerable populations.

Learn more →
🌱

Adolescent Health

Comprehensive adolescent health programs covering sexual and reproductive health, mental wellbeing, and life skills for young people.

Learn more →

Our Vision

A justifiable society with health and economic stability — where every young person and vulnerable community member can live with dignity, opportunity, and hope.

Our Mission

To advocate for youth and other vulnerable groups on health, human rights protection, and economic livelihoods, empowering communities across Northern Uganda to thrive.
